Industry Focus

Energy Generation and Storage

Advanced ceramic solutions for systems that must survive extreme heat, corrosive media, and rigorous electrochemical cycling.

Powering the Future

In the energy sector, materials are often the limiting factor. Whether it is the thermal gradients in a solid oxide fuel cell (SOFC) or the chemical stability required in next generation battery separators, advanced ceramics provide the necessary performance envelope.

Morfion delivers the expertise required to select, test, and optimize ceramic components for critical energy infrastructure.

Application Areas

  • Batteries: High stability separators and solid state electrolytes.
  • Fuel Cells: SOFC interconnects and electrolyte membranes.
  • Capacitors: High dielectric MLCC components for power electronics.
  • Nuclear: Radiation resistant ceramic cladding and shielding.
