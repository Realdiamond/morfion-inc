Core Service

Ceramic 3D Printing

Additive manufacturing for complex ceramic geometries. We design and print components that cannot be produced by conventional means, enabling rapid iteration and novel engineering solutions.

Beyond Conventional Limits

Ceramic 3D printing (Additive Manufacturing) allows for the creation of intricate internal channels, lightweight lattice structures, and topologically optimized components that are impossible to machine or cast.

Our capability spans from functional prototyping to small batch production of mission critical components for aerospace, medical, and industrial applications.

Additive Technologies

  • Digital Light Processing (DLP)
  • Stereolithography (SLA)
  • Robocasting / Direct Ink Writing
  • Binder Jetting Support
