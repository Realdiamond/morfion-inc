Capabilities

Diagnostic Specializations

Comprehensive forensic engineering across all ceramic failure modes and operational environments.

Fracture & Crack Analysis

Fractographic mapping to identify fracture origins, crack propagation paths, and fracture modes under thermal or mechanical load.

Microstructural Defects

SEM and EDS analysis of porosity, grain boundary degradation, secondary phases, and inclusions that compromise mechanical integrity.

Thermal Degradation

Investigation of oxidation, phase transformation, and thermal shock damage in components exposed to cyclic or extreme temperature environments.

Wear & Surface Damage

Tribological assessment of abrasive wear, erosion, and surface fatigue in ceramic components subjected to contact or abrasive environments.

Chemical Corrosion

Compositional analysis of reactive attack, leaching, or chemically induced structural changes using XRD and spectroscopic techniques.

Process-Induced Failures

Tracing defect origins back to sintering, machining, coating, or assembly — with corrective guidance for your manufacturing process.
