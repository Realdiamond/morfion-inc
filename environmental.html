Industry Focus

Environmental Applications

Filtration, remediation, and catalytic support systems using advanced ceramic membranes and structures for a cleaner planet.

Pioneering Clean Process Streams

Advanced ceramics are at the forefront of environmental technology. From high temperature gas filtration to liquid phase separation using ceramic membranes, these materials provide the durability and precision required for sustainable industrial processes.

Morfion delivers the expertise required to select, test, and optimize ceramic components for critical environmental infrastructure.

Application Areas

  • Water Filtration: Porous ceramic membranes for ultrafiltration and desalination.
  • Gas Membranes: Ceramic filters for high temperature gas separation and CO2 capture.
  • Catalytic Support: High surface area ceramic monoliths for industrial emission control.
