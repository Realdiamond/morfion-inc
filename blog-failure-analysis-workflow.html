Failure Analysis in Advanced Ceramics: A Clear Workflow

Evidence first, then corrective action. A systematic approach for high-consequence components.

May 2026 6 min read Failure Analysis

Failure analysis should explain not just what happened, but why it happened and how to prevent a repeat. We use a structured workflow that preserves evidence and links microstructure to service conditions.

1. Preserve evidence

We document the fracture surface and service condition before any destructive prep. This avoids destroying the origin of failure.

2. Identify the origin

Fractography and microstructural imaging isolate the critical flaw and its propagation path.

3. Rebuild the failure chain

We connect design assumptions, process conditions, and loading history to identify the true root cause.

4. Deliver corrective action

The final report includes material, design, and process corrections - not just diagnosis.
