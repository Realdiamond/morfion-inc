Industry Focus

Medical & Bioceramics

Biocompatible ceramic implants and medical device components engineered for regulatory compliance and human safety.

Safety Through Material Integrity

The medical industry requires materials that are not only biocompatible but also exhibit exceptional wear resistance and long term stability. Ceramics are increasingly the material of choice for orthopedic implants and dental applications.

Application Areas

  • Dental Ceramics: High strength zirconia and glass ceramics for restorative dentistry.
  • Bioactive Coatings: Hydroxyapatite and bioactive glass coatings for orthopedic implants.
  • Medical Device Components: Wear resistant ceramic bearings for joint replacement systems.
